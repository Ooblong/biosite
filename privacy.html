What is collected

Only what you type into a form: your email address, and whether you ticked the optional newsletter or marketing boxes. Nothing else is asked for. The sites do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

What it's used for

If you ask for the My Photo Gallery download link, your email is used to send you that link. If you tick the newsletter box, or subscribe through the newsletter form, your email is added to the mailing list for updates. If you tick the marketing box, you may also get emails about future products and offers. Those boxes are always unticked to start.

Who handles it

Emails are sent and the mailing list is stored by an email service provider (Resend) on behalf of . Your email is not sold or shared with anyone else.

Fonts

The sites load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers to fetch them.

Your choices

Newsletter emails include a way to unsubscribe. You can also ask for your email to be removed at any time using the contact details below.
