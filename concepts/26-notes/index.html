Signature: the fog title

The big white headline is sharp at the top and melts into soft blur at the bottom, as if written on a misty window. It's used twice: on the opened note, and as the headline at the top of this page. Each card's own type style does the rest.

How it moves

  • Arrival: the headline un-fogs, the wide product card rises first and its four ticks pop in one by one, then the other cards follow
  • Open a note: the card grows into the full note (a smooth "shared element" morph), and the back button shrinks it home
  • Filter chips: cards that don't match fade out, and the rest glide into their new places
  • Photo stack: the back photos fan out a little when you press the card, hinting that it opens
  • Building pulse: a slow breathing waveform, the only thing that moves on its own
  • Reduced motion: no morphs or cascades; notes open with a plain fade
  • Swipe: inside an open note, swipe sideways to flip to the next note in the stack
  • Escape / back gesture closes the note or sheet; focus returns to the card
  • Deep links: /#gallery opens straight to the product note, handy for an Instagram story link

Fit check

🔵 How it differs from the existing 25

  • 17 Aurora Glass is dark, with moving light and one column. This is light pastel, a two-column note grid, and notes open into their own screen.
  • 18 Bento is a fixed grid of tiles. Here every card is a different kind of note with its own type style, and the cards open, filter and stack.

🟡 Watch-outs

  • White text on pastel can be faint. The headline gets a soft shadow, and all small text stays in dark ink.
  • Frosted blur is heavy for older phones in Instagram's in-app browser. Real blur goes on the dock and sheet only; the cards fake it with milky fills.
  • Fog title must stay readable: only the bottom part blurs, and screen readers get the plain text.
